001 — 004 About the hall

Built for one wedding. Booked for 2,400 more.

Shree Vaibhav Mahal was built in 1998 by the Rao family for their own daughter's wedding — and the neighbours simply never stopped asking for the dates.

Three decades later it is still run by the same family, not a booking agent. What people come back for hasn't changed: a mandap both sides of the family can actually see from their seats, a kitchen on the property so 900 plates go out hot, and enough parking that guests from out of town aren't circling Rajajinagar at 7 PM.

Weddings, receptions, engagements, naming ceremonies, upanayanam, 60th birthdays — if you're feeding more than a hundred people, we've set the hall for it before.
