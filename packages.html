Before you book

Questions families ask us first.

How far in advance should we book?

For November to February muhurthams, 8–10 months. Off-season dates are usually available 6–8 weeks ahead. We hold a date for 7 days without deposit once you've seen the hall.

Can we bring our own caterer?

Yes. There's no outside-catering penalty — you pay hall rent only, and our kitchen is available to them. Most families use ours simply because the food travels ten metres instead of ten kilometres.

What's the advance and cancellation policy?

30% to confirm the date, the balance a week before the function. Cancel more than 90 days out and the advance transfers to any other date within a year.

Is there a mangala snanam / getting-ready room?

The bridal suite on the first floor has an attached bathroom with hot water, a dressing mirror wall and space for the makeup team. Included from the Gold package onwards.

Dates for November to February are going fast.

Call and we'll tell you in one minute whether your muhurtham date is open — no deposit needed to hold a viewing.
